Background

20 years bridging business and language

I spent two decades in the corporate world — as a software developer, leading IT teams, and navigating cross-functional projects — and saw first-hand how misunderstandings between departments, stakeholders and project teams, especially across cultures, led to delays, errors and unnecessary rework.

Even among fluent English speakers, language gaps quietly slow things down. Drawing on that experience, I now help teams and individuals communicate more effectively, particularly when English isn't their first language — so organisations see fewer mistakes, faster progress and better results.

Work in practice

Every engagement is different. Here are a few examples of the kinds of problems clients bring to me — and what changed.

Cross-cultural
collaboration
Bridging communication across cultures

A France-based company faced recurring delays and strained relationships with English-speaking counterparts in the UK, US and Australia. Through tailored group coaching and workshops, their project managers and directors explored tone, politeness strategies, and cultural expectations around hierarchy and deadlines, using real examples from their own experience.

→ Fewer delays, stronger collaboration, and more positive relationships with international partners.
Startup
growth
Helping a French startup win international clients

A small French startup, successful locally, struggled to win business abroad — subtle gaps in vocabulary and confidence made them appear less competent than they were. Coaching focused on sales pitches, high-pressure client questions, and presenting their product with clarity and conviction.

→ Won their first UK client within months, and has since secured several more international contracts.
Career
transition
From engineer to manager

A systems engineer felt stuck, with strong technical skills but a gap in communication, confidence and leadership. Coaching focused on interpersonal skills, interview practice, and presenting his existing leadership experience with clarity to colleagues and senior leaders.

→ Successfully transitioned from developer to manager, and is now a Fellow of the Chartered Management Institute (UK).
