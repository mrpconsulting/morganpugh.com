What I offer

Communication programmes for international business

Practical, focused programmes for technical teams, leaders and startups that need to communicate clearly and confidently with English-speaking clients, colleagues and decision-makers. Every engagement is adapted to the participants, their audience and the real communication challenges they face.

International Communication for Teams
A practical one- or two-day workshop for teams that regularly work with English-speaking clients, colleagues, suppliers or stakeholders. Using examples from your organisation, participants learn to structure information clearly, explain complex subjects to different audiences and communicate with greater confidence.
  • Explain complex or technical subjects more clearly
  • Adapt messages for executives, customers and non-technical audiences
  • Write more concise and effective emails and reports
  • Make decisions, responsibilities and next steps explicit
  • Reduce misunderstandings and unnecessary rework
One or two days
International Pitches & Presentations

How it works

A clear process from first conversation to follow-up

Every engagement begins with a structured discovery call to understand your organisation, your audience and the communication challenges affecting your team.

01

Discovery call

We discuss where communication currently becomes unclear, the business impact this is having and what you would like participants to do differently.

02

Initial assessment

I review the information gathered during the call and, where appropriate, examples of presentations, emails or other communication materials supplied by your team.

03

Recommended programme

You receive a clear proposal setting out the issues identified, the recommended workshop format, intended outcomes, deliverables and cost.

04

Workshop and follow-up

The workshop uses examples and scenarios relevant to your organisation. Afterwards, you receive a summary of the principal findings and recommended next steps, with an optional review session after six to eight weeks.

Bridging communication across cultures

A company working across several international markets faced recurring delays and strained relationships with English-speaking counterparts in the UK, US and Australia. Through tailored group coaching and workshops, their project managers and directors explored tone, politeness strategies, and cultural expectations around hierarchy and deadlines, using real examples from their own experience.

→ Fewer delays, stronger collaboration, and more positive relationships with international partners.
Startup
growth
Helping a startup win international clients

A small startup, successful in its home market, struggled to win business abroad — subtle gaps in vocabulary and confidence made them appear less competent than they were. Coaching focused on sales pitches, high-pressure client questions, and presenting their product with clarity and conviction.

→ Won their first UK client within months, and has since secured several more international contracts.
